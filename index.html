Teacher's Note: Dressing to Express!

Have you noticed how an oversized vintage hoodie gives a totally different vibe than a tight leather jacket? Fashion isn't just about clothes; it's how we express who we are! GRAMMAR HACK

The VIP Line of Adjectives 👑

Hey! Did you know English adjectives are total divas? 💅 They refuse to stand in the wrong order! Imagine saying:
"Look at my denim, black, awesome, baggy jeans!" 🤢
That just hurts the ears! To sound like a native, you need the Secret VIP Code before the noun. Here is who gets into the club first:

1

Opinion

What do you think?
(Awesome, Hideous)

2

Style/Size

What does it look like?
(Baggy, Skinny)

3

Colour

What color is it?
(Black, Checked)

4

Material

What's it made of?
(Denim, Leather)
