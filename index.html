          The Secret VIP Line of Adjectives!

          Imagine telling your friend about your new outfit. You wouldn't say, "Look at my denim, black, awesome, baggy jeans!" That just sounds... weird, right?

          In English, adjectives have a secret VIP order before they hit the noun. When using two or three adjectives, we usually follow this rule: What you think about it comes first, then what it looks like (style/size), then colour, and finally what it's made of (material) right next to the noun.

          Awesome (Opinion) Baggy (Style) Black (Colour) Denim (Material) Jeans! (Noun)
